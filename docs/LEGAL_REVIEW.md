## Sources and consistency checks

- [OSI canonical MIT license](https://opensource.org/license/mit): standard permission and notice requirements.
- [GitHub repository licensing](https://docs.github.com/en/repositories/managing-your-repositorys-settings-and-features/customizing-your-repository/licensing-a-repository): explicit distribution license and standard root location.
- [Open Source Guides — legal considerations](https://opensource.guide/legal/): contribution rights, licensing and separation of branding.
- [Brazilian Software Law, Law 9,609/1998](https://www.planalto.gov.br/ccivil_03/leis/l9609.htm): software rights and licensing; registration is not a prerequisite to protection. Commercial obligations cannot be waived simply by a project notice.
- [Brazilian Consumer Protection Code](https://www.planalto.gov.br/ccivil_03/leis/l8078compilado.htm): mandatory protections and invalid abusive clauses where applicable. Terms contain a mandatory-rights savings clause, not blanket immunity.
- [LGPD, Law 13,709/2018](https://www.planalto.gov.br/ccivil_03/_ato2015-2018/2018/lei/l13709.htm): privacy boundaries; no claim of universal compliance or blanket consent for future collection.

The documents are in English for the public repository. Any future consumer-facing commercial agreement in Brazil needs audience-appropriate, understandable presentation and review for its actual service, operator, jurisdiction and data flows.
