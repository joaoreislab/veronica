# Licensing and notices review — October 9, 2026

## Decision and scope

The maintainer requested a license and terms for the existing public project. MIT was selected for code and associated documentation, matching free distribution, commercial integration and optional services around the project. The canonical license text is unmodified apart from the year and copyright holder. A matching copy accompanies the installable skill folder.

Terms, a privacy notice, contribution guidance and separate asset notices supplement the license. They do not add restrictions to MIT. This is a documentation-only publication: runtime version 1.2.0, existing tags and release assets remain unchanged. Users of historical packages should obtain the current notices when redistributing MIT-covered material; those old packages did not include the new license file. No active installation is replaced by this publication.

## Evidence inspected

- Repository source imports and distribution files: standard-library and project-local runtime dependencies; no bundled external framework identified.
- `veronica/references/origens.md`: recorded independent implementation and linked design influences, including fixed source references for the efficiency work.
- Existing workflows: externally invoked GitHub Actions, not incorporated runtime code.
- Current helper source: local project/token records and no built-in adoption telemetry endpoint.
- Poster: maintainer-supplied Gemini-named image; no independent rights clearance evidence. It is expressly excluded from MIT, not asserted to be exclusively owned or universally cleared.

This inspection is not an exhaustive similarity scan, a title opinion or verification of every historical contribution. The grant extends only to rights the respective holders can grant. New dependencies, copied material, hosted services or commercial contracts require their own review.

## Sources and consistency checks

- [OSI canonical MIT license](https://opensource.org/license/mit): standard permission and notice requirements.
- [GitHub repository licensing](https://docs.github.com/en/repositories/managing-your-repositorys-settings-and-features/customizing-your-repository/licensing-a-repository): explicit distribution license and standard root location.
- [Open Source Guides — legal considerations](https://opensource.guide/legal/): contribution rights, licensing and separation of branding.
- [Brazilian Software Law, Law 9,609/1998](https://www.planalto.gov.br/ccivil_03/leis/l9609.htm): software rights and licensing; registration is not a prerequisite to protection. Commercial obligations cannot be waived simply by a project notice.
- [Brazilian Consumer Protection Code](https://www.planalto.gov.br/ccivil_03/leis/l8078compilado.htm): mandatory protections and invalid abusive clauses where applicable. Terms contain a mandatory-rights savings clause, not blanket immunity.
- [LGPD, Law 13,709/2018](https://www.planalto.gov.br/ccivil_03/_ato2015-2018/2018/lei/l13709.htm): privacy boundaries; no claim of universal compliance or blanket consent for future collection.

The documents are in English for the public repository. Any future consumer-facing commercial agreement in Brazil needs audience-appropriate, understandable presentation and review for its actual service, operator, jurisdiction and data flows.

## Limits

This publication does not establish a paid service, approve future telemetry or guarantee clearance of the supplied artwork.
